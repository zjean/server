v2 app chrome — background options

The chrome rendered under each candidate ground. Every contrast figure on this page is computed from the hex values shown, by WCAG 2.x relative luminance — nothing here is typed by hand.

This page records the decision as of 2026-08-05. The cool slate (hue-slate / Option 2 below) is what shipped from that decision and is what _tokens.scss now ships. "Current" below no longer means current — it is relabelled "Warm neutral (pre-2026-08-05)" and kept only as the baseline every other option was measured against.
One measurement worth reading first. The surfaces shipping today are not brown — all seven measure 0.003–0.008 chroma in OKLab, i.e. effectively neutral. The warmth lives in the type and line tokens, which are two to four times more chromatic (--si-fg-muted 0.014 · --si-fg-tertiary 0.013 · --si-fg is a cream, not a white). So rotating only the seven bg tokens is a nearly invisible change. Every option below moves the type ramp with the ground.

And one constraint. The top of the ramp has 0.02 of lightness headroom: the focus ring clears SC 1.4.11's 3:1 only up to a surface at L=0.348, and bg6 is L=0.328 today. Lightening the overlay surfaces breaks the focus ring and every tertiary glyph on menus, dialogs and tooltips — silently, with no build failure. That is the whole reason options 2 and 3 differ.
